## Script (about 35 seconds)

Say it in your own words. Delivery matters more than exact wording.

**HOOK (0–3s)** *(on-screen text: "How long does your vial really last?")*
> If you've got a vial of Reta sitting in your fridge right now, there's one thing you need to know.

**SETUP (3–8s)**
> Storage is simple: cold, dry, and out of the light. The detail most people miss is that the powder and the mixed vial follow different rules.

**POWDER (8–16s)** *(on-screen text: "POWDER: fridge 36–46°F · freezer for long-term")*
> Still in powder form? Keep it in the fridge at 36 to 46 degrees. Not touching it for a while? The freezer is your long-term option. Unmixed is where it's most stable.

**THE TURN (16–19s)** *(beat, lean into the camera)*
> But the second you add bacteriostatic water, the clock starts.

**MIXED (19–27s)** *(on-screen text: "MIXED: fridge only · don't freeze · check the label")*
> Once it's mixed, it lives in the fridge. No freezer, and not in the door where the temperature swings. Follow the timeline on your label. Mixed vials don't last anywhere near as long as powder.

**RED FLAGS (27–32s)** *(on-screen text: "Cloudy? Discolored? Particles? Toss it.")*
> If it ever looks cloudy, changes color, or has anything floating in it, don't use it. Toss it.

**CTA (32–36s)**
