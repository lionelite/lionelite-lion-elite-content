## Claims he makes (checked before we reuse anything)

- Powder, refrigerated at 36–46°F: he says 1–2 years. *Varies by product; defer to the label/COA.*
- Powder, frozen: he says 2–5 years. *Too specific to repeat; we say "longest-term storage" instead.*
- After mixing with bacteriostatic water, refrigerated: he says 90–120 days. **Don't repeat this.** Common guidance for reconstituted peptides is much shorter, around 4 weeks. It's the claim most likely to get challenged.
- Cloudy, discolored or particles means don't use it. *Fine to reuse.*
